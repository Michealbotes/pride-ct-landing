The Road to 2028

Pride in Cape Town, made bigger than ever.

Cape Town has always marched with heart — and in 2028 the city hosts WorldPride, the first ever on African soil. We're an independent crew of locals building a network of events, products and partnerships across the city in the run-up — growing the community, the culture and the celebration.

This page is where it starts. We'll be sharing the highlights beyond the main events — the parties, pop-ups, people and places — to make WorldPride in Cape Town an even greater experience. Follow along as line-ups, launches and collaborations are announced.

Events

Parties, markets, screenings and community gatherings across the Mother City — a growing calendar leading up to the main celebration.

Products & Collabs

Limited drops and local collaborations that carry the flag — made in Cape Town, made with pride.

Community

A network of people, venues and organisations across the city, connected and amplified. Everyone's invited.

What to do this Pride

Taste, travel and celebrate the Mother City.

A growing line-up of experiences from our network — here's what's brewing for this Pride season.

Gay Wine Tour

A queer-friendly day through the Cape Winelands — tastings, mountain views and very good company.

African Wine Farm Braai

Fire, wine and the Winelands — a proper South African braai on the farm, Pride edition.

Travel Tours

Guided trips around the Mother City and beyond — Cape Point, the Peninsula and the spots only locals know.

Pop-Up Dinner

An intimate pop-up evening with a three-course dinner — new venue, new menu, every time.

Restaurants

Our pick of queer-friendly places to eat and drink across the city, updated as we go.

Hikes

Community hikes on the mountain — Lion's Head sunrises, Table Mountain trails, all paces welcome.

Park Run

Saturday morning park runs with the crew — 5km, zero pressure, coffee and catch-ups after.

Sunset Cruises

Golden-hour cruises from the waterfront — sea air, bubbles and the mountain glowing from the water.
